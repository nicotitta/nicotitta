## Nicolò Tittarelli

I am an MSc student in Cybersecurity Engineering at Politecnico di Torino, with a BSc in Computer Science from the University of Perugia. 
CyberChallenge.IT fueled my interest in security, and I keep practicing through CTFs and boot-to-root machines across binary exploitation, cryptography, web exploitation and network security.
The repositories here are university projects: embedded systems (QEMU board emulation with FreeRTOS), systems programming in C, and web and distributed-systems development.
I’m especially drawn to low-level and embedded security, where hardware, firmware and memory behaviour decide how systems fail.
