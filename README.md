## Nicolò Tittarelli

I am an MSc student in Cybersecurity Engineering at Politecnico di Torino, with a BSc in Computer Science from the University of Perugia. My interest in the cybersecurity field was sparked by the CyberChallenge.IT competition and since then I have always enjoyed tackling CTF challenges and boot-to-root machines, and I'm always open to studying and practicing whatever area of cybersecurity catches my interest, ranging from from binary exploitation to cryptography, web exploitation and network security. I like the process of learning a new technique and then immediately putting it to the test against a real challenge.

My repositories contain some of the projects I have worked on during my univeristy studies, covering offensive security practice (web exploitation writeups along with python code scripts), software engineering projects (web application emulating Whatsapp built with javascript) and distributed systems work.
